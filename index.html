Family Corner

A plain-language guide for families.

What MindUrMind is

MindUrMind is a school-based wellbeing awareness and self-learning tool. It helps students notice emotions, practise calm skills, build healthy habits, and choose small kind actions.

What MindUrMind is not

It is not a diagnosis tool, not therapy, not a surveillance platform, and not a replacement for support from parents, guardians, teachers, counselors, or health professionals.

Why there is no parent login

Private reflections, notes, and the AI wellbeing coach are designed to stay on-device only. The school view shows only local aggregate trends, not individual student records.

Privacy in simple words

Private student notes, history, habits, and chat stay in this browser on this device. School view does not show student names, journals, or individual profiles.

Conversation tips for families

Start gently
“How has your week felt?” works better than pressure-filled questions.
Listen before solving
A calm response helps students feel safer speaking honestly.
Keep it practical
Try sleep routine support, screen boundaries, meals, movement, and one tiny next step.
Seek support when needed
If concerns persist, talk to a school counselor, trusted adult, or suitable professional support service.

About & Privacy

How the app is positioned and how data works.

Positioning statement

MindUrMind is a school-based wellbeing awareness and self-learning tool. It supports emotional awareness, coping skills, healthy habits, peaceful actions, and student privacy.

What data is stored

Daily check-ins, private notes, habit tracking, eco-actions, news action steps, kindness actions, AI chat history, Emotion Quest progress, and mini-game activity can be saved in local browser storage.

What is device-only

Private notes, journal-style text, AI wellbeing coach messages, history, and student-side personal activity stay on-device only in this browser unless changed in a future approved version.

What school can see

Only local, non-identifiable trends on the same device/browser, such as mood distribution, average sleep, average focused study minutes, and counts of positive actions. No names, IDs, journals, or chat.

Future cloud sync possibility

MindUrMind stores private notes on-device only. If the school approves, cloud sync can be enabled only for aggregated, non-identifiable wellbeing trends, such as weekly mood distribution and average sleep hours by grade. No individual student data, names, IDs, or notes will be uploaded.

Safety disclaimer

This app does not diagnose or replace urgent support. If a student feels unsafe or thinks they might harm themselves, they should stop using the app and tell a trusted adult immediately and contact local emergency or helpline support.
